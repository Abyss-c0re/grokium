   Hot-path SoT is **0/1 cells, NEXUS_COORD flags, SMX bits, algocube digits**.  
   Labels and prose are annotations only.

   Free-form ideology, activism, brand campaigns, hive-mind slogans, and  
   off-station “must believe” prose **shall not** enter StateMatrix, SMX stream,  
   nanobot raw, LOVR SoT fold, or NEXUS_COORD plates.

3. **Sanitize every input gate.**  
   Session edge may use verbal tools under Commander; before anything touches  
   the Cube bus, inputs are reduced to **flags / bits / allowlisted keys**  
   or **rejected fail-closed**.

4. **WiVRn / LOVR / Magisk / OS are ways — not pulpits.**  
   Streaming and XR carriers carry **frames and pose**, not political narrative.  
   No telemetry. No collection. No silent cloud morality pack.

5. **Hivemind means matrix unity — not propaganda.**  
   Nanobot unity = SoT agreement across peers. It is **not** a channel for  
   external social messaging.
